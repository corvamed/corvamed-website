Corvamed in one minute

The idea, the rules we keep and the road ahead.

Text of the video

Somewhere near you, a new medicine is being tested. It might help you, or someone you love. But you would probably never hear about it. And if you did, the rules for who can take part are written for specialists.

Corvamed wants to change that. You describe your situation in plain words. Our AI reads the trial rules, turns them into clear facts, and shows trials that may fit you: what is being tested, where, what it asks of you, and the risks.

Then the choice is yours. Nothing is shared without your consent, and the trial doctor always decides.

Corvamed will be free for patients, and it will show every suitable trial, not only those whose sponsors pay.

We are at the very beginning. If you are a patient, a doctor, a research team or an investor, write to us at corvamed.com.
